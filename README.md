## Aim:
To identify user problems, write UX problem statements, select suitable research methods, and create user personas for different UX project scenarios.

## Algorithm:

## :UX Project Scenarios
Scenario 1: Health App
1. Identifying and Writing Problem Statements
A health app is designed to help people keep track of their medicines, appointments, and health records.

User pain points:
People sometimes forget to take their medicines. It can be difficult to keep track of appointments. Medical records may be stored in different places. Older people may struggle with complicated app designs.

Problem Statement 1:
Patients need a simple way to remember their medicines because they may forget the correct time to take them, which can lead to missed doses.

Problem Statement 2:
Patients need an easy way to access their medical records because their information may be stored in different places, which makes managing their health difficult.

2. Research Methods
Interviews: We can talk directly to patients and understand their problems and expectations.

Surveys: Surveys can be used to collect responses from many people and understand common problems.

Usability Testing: Users can try the app while we observe where they face difficulties.

Diary Study: Users can record their daily medicine-taking habits, which helps us understand their routine.

Best methods: Interviews and Usability Testing

These methods are useful because interviews help us understand what users actually need, while usability testing helps us find problems when they use the app.

3. User Personas
Persona 1 – Lakshmi
Age: 65 Occupation: Retired Teacher Goals: Take medicines on time and manage appointments easily. Behaviors: Uses her phone for basic tasks and prefers simple apps. Pain Points: Often forgets medicine timings and finds complicated apps confusing. Motivation: Wants to manage her health without depending on others.

Persona 2 – Arjun
Age: 32 Occupation: Software Engineer Goals: Manage appointments and access health information quickly. Behaviors: Uses mobile apps regularly and prefers quick solutions. Pain Points: Has a busy schedule and finds it difficult to organize medical information. Motivation: Wants an easy way to manage his health while working.

Scenario 2: E-Commerce Website 🛒
An e-commerce website allows users to search for products, compare them, and purchase them online.

1. Identifying and Writing Problem Statements
User pain points:

Users may find it difficult to find the product they want. There are often too many products to choose from. A long checkout process can be annoying. Users may not get clear information about delivery.

Problem Statement 1:
Online shoppers need an easy way to find the right products because there are so many choices available, which can make them confused and frustrated.

Problem Statement 2:
Online shoppers need a quick checkout process because filling in too many details takes time, which may cause them to leave without completing the purchase.

2. Research Methods
Surveys: We can ask users about their shopping habits and what problems they usually face.

Interviews: Talking to users helps us understand why they choose certain products or leave a website.

Usability Testing: Users can be asked to search for a product and complete a purchase while we observe their difficulties.

Website Analytics: Analytics can show where users leave the website, such as during product search or checkout.

Best methods: Surveys and Usability Testing

Surveys help us understand common problems among many shoppers, while usability testing helps us find actual problems in the website's design.

3. User Personas
Persona 1 – Ananya
Age: 20 Occupation: College Student Goals: Find good products at affordable prices. Behaviors: Compares prices, checks reviews, and looks for discounts. Pain Points: Confusing navigation, expensive products, and lengthy checkout. Motivation: Wants good-quality products within her budget.

Persona 2 – Rahul
Age: 29 Occupation: Marketing Executive Goals: Buy products quickly and get them delivered on time. Behaviors: Usually shops through his phone and prefers familiar brands. Pain Points: Too many choices and unclear delivery information. Motivation: Wants a fast and hassle-free shopping experience.
